
## Plan length discounts (UPI checkout)
- Monthly 0%, Quarterly (3 months) 6%, Yearly (12 months) 8%. Yearly always saves more than quarterly.
- Super Admin discount codes: 1–50% off, applied after the length saving and before GST; one use per society.
- Creating a society starts with choosing a plan, then setup, then payment/trial.
- Renewal reminders to society admins 7, 3, 1 days before, on the day, and 1 day after the plan/trial ends.
