
## Plan length discounts (UPI checkout)
- Monthly 0%, Quarterly (3 months) 6%, Yearly (12 months) 8%. Yearly always saves more than quarterly.
- Super Admin discount codes: 1–50% off, applied after the length saving and before GST; one use per society.
- Creating a society starts with choosing a plan, then setup, then payment/trial.
- Renewal reminders to society admins 7, 3, 1 days before, on the day, and 1 day after the plan/trial ends.

## Plan contents (Oct 2026)
- Starter: core, billing, notices, help desk, AI Secretary + AI Digest. No visitors/guard/vehicles/amenities/polls/feed; Team & Roles without guard logins.
- Growth: Starter + visitors, guard entry, vehicles, amenities, polls, community feed, finance tools. Has ads.
- Pro: Growth + Advanced Automation, meetings, AGM, formal votes, elections, branding, no ads.
- Term prices per flat are whole rupees: 6% quarterly / 8% yearly, rounded (Starter ₹24→₹23/qtr, ₹96→₹88/yr). Keep discounts under 12%.
